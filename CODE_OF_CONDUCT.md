# OpenWork Code of Conduct

We want a respectful and supportive environment for contributors of all backgrounds, identities, professions, and experience levels.

## We expect

- Respectful, patient, constructive communication.
- Specific feedback about work rather than personal attacks.
- Welcoming attitudes toward beginner questions.
- Consideration for volunteer time and boundaries.
- Honest attribution and responsible handling of open-source licenses.
- Protection of private and confidential information.

## We do not permit

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, intimidation, bullying, personal attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, retaliation, or persistent disruption. People must not be pressured into unpaid commercial work or misled about OpenWork offering paid jobs, internships, or credentials.

## Scope

These standards apply to OpenWork repositories, official discussions, collaboration spaces, and public conduct representing OpenWork.

## Reports and enforcement

**Do not submit private or identifying conduct reports as public issues.** On GitHub, use the platform's built-in abuse/content reporting mechanisms when applicable. A dedicated private reporting contact and appeal process must be configured **before broad contributor onboarding**.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, issue warnings, limit participation, or escalate serious conduct to appropriate services. Actions should prioritize safety, proportionality, confidentiality, and protection from retaliation.

These guidelines will be refined transparently as the community develops.
